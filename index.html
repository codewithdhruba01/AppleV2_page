Aerospace Precision

Folded to perfection.
Hinge re-engineered from the atom up.

To create a foldable iPhone worthy of the name, our engineers crafted a revolutionary dual-synchronized gear mechanism in Grade 5 titanium. Four interlocking micro-pinions guide the ultra-thin flexible glass into an optimized teardrop profile, eliminating screen creases completely.

When closed, both halves sit completely flush with zero gap. When opened, high-tensile locking detents hold the canvas perfectly flat at 180 degrees — or anywhere in between with fluid flex mode.

Interactive Hinge Angle: 135°
0° (Closed) 90° (Flex Stand) 180° (Canvas)
Current state: Studio Angle (Ergonomic Reading)
Dual Super Retina XDR

Two screens.
Zero compromises.

On the outside: a sharp, edge-to-edge 6.3-inch OLED cover display with Dynamic Island, Ceramic Shield 2, and Always-On display for rapid one-handed navigation.

Unfold to reveal an expansive 7.9-inch flexible OLED canvas. Powered by ProMotion technology adaptive from 1Hz to 120Hz, reaching 3,000 nits outdoor peak brightness with anti-reflective nano-texture coating.

Run Final Cut Pro on the top half while monitoring color grades and notes on the lower canvas with zero lag.
Apple Silicon

A19 Pro Bionic.
Silicon designed for dual-screen power.

Built on second-generation 3-nanometer architecture, the A19 Pro features a 6-core CPU with 2 performance cores and 4 efficiency cores, a 6-core pro GPU with hardware-accelerated ray tracing, and a 16-core Neural Engine processing 40 trillion operations per second.

Apple Intelligence runs privately on device: summarize long document threads, rewrite emails in your chosen tone, clean up photobombers with one tap, or ask Siri to navigate across both open apps simultaneously.

Pro Camera System

48MP across every dimension.
Flex mode changes how you shoot.

A triple fusion optical system engineered directly into a slim folding profile. With the unique hinge design, you can set iPhone Duo on any flat surface in flex mode for shake-free astrophotography, hands-free group selfies using the main 48MP sensor, or overhead desk shots.

24mm Fusion: 48MP quad-pixel sensor with f/1.78 aperture and second-generation sensor-shift optical image stabilization.
